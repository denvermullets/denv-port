## My 2024 Wrapped

first off, let me just say that everyone has their own path and struggles and your value is not determined by your output. that being said, i was looking back at my 2024 and was kind of surprised to see all that i accomplished. in september i had my first child (beau) so the past couple months have been a blur and i expect my 2025 output to dip a little, ha. also, i'm in a phase where i want to build my own tools even if they exist as a SaaS/PaaS, mostly as an exercise of figuring out how things work. the more complex the better.

#### not including my day job, the things i built in 2024 were:

- (freelance contract) a knowledge base tracker
- (freelance contract) a criminal background check app
- _BallotBox_ - mostly a MVP demo of an embeddable Voting widget w/it's own Voter based platform where each user is able to create/close their own Ballots, keep track of previous votes and see activity of others. When making a Vote you can choose to Amplify your point winnings by consuming your existing points
  - https://voteballotbox.com/
  - demo of it embedded - https://whenthemusicstops.net/forums/music/punk-hardcore-post-hardcore/mewithoutyou
  - built with React and Ruby on Rails
- _When the Music Stops_ - a super minimal music forum. i just miss the era of smaller forums and real community building that doesn't involve talking to bots/AI. i casually aimed to mimic the old phpbb forum feel with embed codes, but other than that it is realllly meant to be barebones.
  - https://whenthemusicstops.net/
  - built with Ruby on Rails
  - i have a lot of plans for this to expand in 2025
- _99 Staples_ - a couple years ago i built this Magic The Gathering app and wanted to rewrite it in just Rails while giving it an updated design. it keeps and updated list of all cards and prices as well as letting users track their own cards in their collections.
  - over the holiday break i started working on a little raspberry pi scanner for the cards that handles adding cards to my collection on 99 staples, not done yet but this will be a thing finished this year
  - updated app should be live this weekend and lots of other plans for this to expand in 2025
- _Denvermullets Analytics_ - i wanted to have my own mini Mixpanel-esque tracker w/a dashboard. it's kinda basic now but will be expanded on in 2025
- i started working on a video game that is being built in DragonRuby and chose to dive in and create my own ECS system, something i had not really used before. hoping to wrap this up in 2025 as well

if you've made it this far then i appreciate you more than you can know. i've got a few other ideas and really just wish that i had more time to execute on them. maybe one of these will grow into a real thing at some point, who knows. also, as an aside, you probably noticed most of my 2024 stuff was in ruby/rails and that's largely because i think rails 7 and rails 8 is really good and super fun - but also that my day job is mostly JS based. i just like to stay busy and learning as much as i can by building.

i'd love to hear / see things you've built if you'd like to share!
